                AR window

                How it works

                Hold the phone up to the window. The rear camera shows the view with targeting-pod boxes on landmarks, airports, your destination, top of descent and your marks, each with distance, bearing and a short note. Arrows at the edges point to things off to the left or right. Tap a box to lock it.

                How it works
                A box shows where something should be, sized by how sure the phone is of its heading. Fused (default) uses the compass for where the phone points and your GPS track to correct its drift, so boxes stay tight (about ±1.5°) and widen by themselves if the compass reports trouble or drifts away from the GPS. If boxes sit off, tap Align while looking square out of the window, or fine-tune with Trim. TV, GRN and WHT switch the camera look. Things beyond your horizon get dashed boxes. Entertainment only, not for navigation.

                                  Data source
                                  How it works
                                  Live positions come from adsb.lol, a free community ADS-B network; if adsb.lol doesn't have the aircraft, Cabin Panel's server asks adsb.fi (second feed) and the tile says which one answered. Its API doesn't allow requests straight from a web page, so Cabin Panel asks its own private proxy, so your lookups aren't shared with other people's. adsb.lol still allows about 500 lookups a day per proxy address. Only if the private proxy fails does it use the free public cors.dev relay, which is shared with other users and can see which aircraft you look up; untick above to never use it. An override URL must take the full adsb.lol address after it (https://your-proxy/https://api.adsb.lol/…). Checks slow down when the aircraft is far away and drop to every 5 minutes while the app is in the background.
                                  How it works
                                  Display only: tracking never changes your own flight, phases or logbook. ETA, delay and weather are estimates, not airline information. Aircraft with ADS-B off, outside receiver coverage or hidden from public feeds can't be shown. Your departure time is local time at your airport. ADS-B data: adsb.lol (ODbL). Aircraft lookups: adsbdb.com, hexdb.io. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com.

                                                How it works
                                                HUD scene: on Auto the background follows the real sun (night, dusk or day), switches to ocean over water and to a runway view on approach. The scenes are illustrations rendered in Blender, not real terrain.
                                                Window seat: pick Left or Right (facing forward) and "Coming up" lists only what will pass your side, plus anything near the track ahead. Landmark voice callouts follow the same setting.
                                                Out the window: sun times allow for your altitude letting you see further over the horizon. Landmark positions are approximate, and visibility depends on cloud.
                                                Attitude, GPS mode: bank comes from how fast your track is turning (assuming a coordinated turn) and pitch from your climb angle plus about 2.5°. It needs groundspeed above about 60 kt. Gyro mode: stand the phone upright facing you, braced so it can't move, and tap Level in level flight. It catches quick changes well but drifts back toward level in long turns.
                                                Uses your phone's GPS, which keeps working in flight mode. Sit at a window for a fix. Altitude is GPS height above sea level, not the pressure altitude the crew flies. Not for navigation.
